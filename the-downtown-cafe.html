Yes, free delivery above Rs 499 anywhere in Patna. WhatsApp or call us to place your order.
Is the food pure vegetarian?
A fully vegetarian kitchen. No onion and garlic options are also available on request.
Do you take party orders?
Bulk and party orders for 20 plus guests are welcome. Give us one day of notice so everything arrives hot.

We are open daily from 11 AM to 10 PM, including Sundays and holidays.
